Forty years of trust. A site that looks half-built.

Forty years in bespoke pools is a genuinely rare claim, and recognised supplier names on the page back it up. But the site itself has literal placeholder text still live: "Slide title," "Write your caption here," generic "Button" labels in the carousel. There are no reviews shown anywhere, no trade accreditation badge, and no way to tell a serious enquiry from a browser. For a business that says it gets plenty of enquiries but only wants the right ones, the site currently has no way to tell the difference.

The stated problem is not too few enquiries, it is too many of the wrong kind. Without a form that asks budget, timeline, and project type before someone reaches the phone, every enquiry costs the same amount of time to qualify by hand, tyre-kickers and real buyers alike. The fix here is a filter, not more traffic, and it is one of the cheapest things on this whole page to build.

Forty years of reputation already exists. Filtering the enquiries that already arrive is the fast, low-cost win here.

Priority Roadmap - Fix First
  • Remove the placeholder text and generic button labels site-wide
  • A real enquiry form with qualifying questions: project type, budget range, timeline
  • Show the Yell and Houzz reviews on the site itself, not just third-party pages
  • Instant reply on every enquiry, so a serious buyer never waits to hear back
Build Next - Compounding Growth
  • Trade accreditation displayed clearly if held, or pursued if not, buyers at this price point look for it
  • Service-area pages across the eight counties already claimed
  • A simple review-request step added to the servicing and aftercare side of the business
  • Tracking already exists (GTM) - wire it up before spending anything on ads

Forty years of proof. A form that cannot tell who is serious.

The fastest fix here is not more enquiries. It is knowing which ones are worth the call.
